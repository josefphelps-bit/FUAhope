Acknowledgment of Receipt and Agreement to Terms

1. Individual Grant:

- The funds allocated to your card represents an individual grant funded solely by Hope Charities.

2. Prepaid Debit Card Use:

- This card works like a gift card. Any remaining balance must be paid using another payment method, once you exhaust the daily allotment. (e.g., cash, personal credit card).

EX: If a meal costs $60 and the card balance is $50, I must inform the server to charge $50 to the card and cover the remaining $10 with another personal form of payment.

3. Daily Use:

- Any remaining balance on the card at the end of the day will be removed, and a new balance will be added the following day, assuming all eligibility terms and conditions have been satisfied.

4. Use of Scholarship Funds:

- The prepaid debit card is provided for the sole purpose of covering your evening meal on the date the disbursement is made into your Prepaid Debit Card. No other expenses are deemed eligible for payment with this grant.

5. Participation Requirement:

- Hope Charities has made significant efforts to offer this scholarship, selecting you from among many applicants. You are expected to attend each and every session available during the conference, to maintain your eligibility for this grant.

- If I am unable to attend due to illness or an unforeseen circumstance, I will notify the conference staff promptly. Exceptions will be made in such cases.

6. Return of Debit Card:

- Your card will be canceled at the end of the event; please return your debit card to the Info Central Station.

I acknowledge that I have received the prepaid debit card and understand the terms and expectations outlined above. I further agree to participate in conference sessions and responsibly use the card as set forth in this Participation Agreement.
